01 / Overview & Philosophy

I am an Engineering Tech Lead & Senior Backend Software Engineer with a decade of experience designing resilient distributed systems, modernizing monolithic platforms, and mentoring engineering teams.

My engineering approach centers on Domain-Driven Design (DDD), pragmatic observability, automated quality gates, and clear architectural documentation. Whether introducing Transactional Outbox relays for mission-critical clinical data or scaling high-throughput message consumers to 10M+ events per day, I optimize for maintainability and observable behavior in production.

Away from the terminal, I am sportive to the bone—training in CrossFit, running, and trekking through the mountains around Rieti. I also unwind by playing guitar and piano, cooking, watching movies, and reading.
